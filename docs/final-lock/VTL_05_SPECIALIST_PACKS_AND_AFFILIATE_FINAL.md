## Activation Rule

Affiliate does not activate at the annual-access layer.

Affiliate only activates:

- after the user is already inside the registered member layer
- after the user owns a qualifying specialist pack
- after compliance checks pass

This prevents the system from turning into a low-trust referral loop.

## Compliance Gates Before Affiliate Activation

- valid paid member state
- valid paid specialist-pack state
- identity confirmed
- no active refund abuse
- no fraud or spam flags

## Direct Referral Rule

Affiliate must stay:

- direct referral only
- no multi-tier commissions
- no MLM structures
- no forced recruiting language

## Homepage vs Pack Commerce Rule

- homepage annual access has its own pricing schedule and no default affiliate
- specialist packs are the commercial depth layer
- pack affiliate rules live only inside the specialist-pack system

## Safe Sales Language

Do say:

- `mở sâu theo chuyên đề`
- `đi vào đúng vấn đề bạn đang cần`
- `mỗi gói có outcome, proof direction, và depth riêng`

Do not say:

- `mua một gói là kiếm tiền ngay`
- `ai cũng bán được`
- `hoa hồng tự bật ở lớp public`
- `chỉ cần giới thiệu người là có thu nhập`
