2. Deep and professional content must route back to the homepage purchase hub.
3. Homepage annual access pricing is now:
   - year 1: `9 USD/year`
   - year 2: `66 USD/year`
   - year 3 onward: `99 USD/year`
4. This annual access rule applies to the homepage product only.
5. Specialist depth is sold through 9 separate packs.
6. Affiliate activation starts at the specialist-pack layer, not at the public registration layer.
7. Every team must complete the planning files before development.
8. This pack is the final locked version until the founder explicitly unlocks it.
