## 0. Purpose

This file is the **public language and trust layer**. It tells the system:

- what the brand says and does not say in public,
- what the brand stands for (tagline, Vietnam Advantage),
- what 5 commitments the brand makes to every member,
- which legal regimes the public surface must obey,
- which words must never appear in public,
- which words must always appear in public when the underlying concept is mentioned,
- where the boundary is between **public surface** (no login required) and **post-login surface** (income figures, contributor terms, invest education).

Implementation contracts (DIM §7-§11), product contracts (`VETUONGLAI_PRODUCT_AND_MEMBERSHIP_ARCHITECTURE_2026.md`), contributor contracts (`VETUONGLAI_CONTRIBUTOR_ECONOMY_AND_EARNING_POLICY_2026.md`), and copy-block sequencing (`VETUONGLAI_DOCS_AND_HOMEPAGE_PUBLIC_COPY_PLAN_2026.md`) all read **from** this file.

## 1. The brand statement (canonical)

### 1.1 Brand name

- Brand: **vetuonglai** (lowercase, single token, no diacritics in code).
- Display VI: **Vetuonglai** (capitalized first letter only, no diacritics).
- Display EN: **Vetuonglai**.
- Vietnamese reading aid (footer or about page only): "*vé tương lai*" — used as a reading hint, never as a literal product description in marketing.
- Domain canonical: `vetuonglai.com`. Forbidden: `vetuonglai.vn`, `vetuonglai.io`, `invest.iai.one`. Any reference to `iai.one` in public copy is forbidden.

### 1.2 Tagline (locked, single source)

- **VI:** Học đúng. Làm thật. Chứng minh giá trị.
- **EN:** Learn well. Build real. Verify value.

Used as: homepage hero subtitle (under the brand wordmark), `docs.vetuonglai.com` footer tagline, OG image strapline, member welcome email H1, App Store / Play Store description first line, Twitter/X bio, LinkedIn About first paragraph.

Never used as: pricing page header, invest page header (invest has its own neutral education tagline — see §8.4), legal page header (legal pages have no tagline at all), checkout page (no marketing copy on checkout).

No alternative wording is allowed in any localization. Translations into other languages must preserve the three-verb structure (learn / make-real / prove-value) and must not introduce monetary, lifestyle, or aspirational vocabulary.

### 1.3 What the brand does

Vetuonglai is an **operating system for personal capability development**: members learn one practical skill at a time on `edu.*`, prove the skill produced real output through Verified Credentials on `vc.*`, optionally publish or contribute through `read.*` and (V3+) Contributor Studio, and grow a documented record of work that money may follow as a consequence — never as a promise.

This sentence is the canonical "what we do" statement. Public copy may shorten it but may not contradict its three-step ordering: **learn → prove → contribute**. Money is consequence, never the headline.

### 1.4 What the brand does not do

- Vetuonglai is not an affiliate program.
- Vetuonglai is not a multi-level marketing system.
- Vetuonglai is not a get-rich-quick or passive-income system.
- Vetuonglai is not a marketplace in the open-trade sense; the only paid contributor surface is the curated **Contributor Studio** (V3, single-tier referral, ≤15% platform fee).
- Vetuonglai is not an investment platform; `invest.vetuonglai.com` is education-only at the public layer and a gated post-90-day surface for members who meet 7 hard conditions (see §8 + product architecture doc).
- Vetuonglai does not sell, broker, or rent member data.

Each of these "is not" statements may be quoted directly on `docs.vetuonglai.com/about/` or in the FAQ, and must be quoted whenever a regulator, journalist, or partner asks the question.

## 2. The 5 red lines (canonical commitments)

These are the public-facing commitments. They are the public version of the longer internal rule lists in `FTC §1`, `DPC §3`, `IGR §0`, and `RFM §1.3`. Public copy uses these 5 — no more, no fewer — when expressing the brand's commitments to members.

### 2.1 Không hứa thu nhập / No income promise

Vetuonglai does not promise income, does not show "earn $X / month" headlines, does not use the words "đổi đời", "passive income", "đảm bảo thu nhập", "get rich", or any equivalent. Historical contributor income figures may be shown only inside post-login surfaces alongside an Income Disclaimer, never on a public surface or above the fold of any page.

### 2.2 Không referral đa cấp / Single-tier referral only

Every referral arrangement on Vetuonglai is single-tier. There is no level 2, no downline, no commission tree, no recruitment incentive. This is a permanent rule, derived from FTC anti-pyramid guidance and Vietnam Nghị định 40/2018.

### 2.3 Không thu phí trẻ em dưới 18 trực tiếp / Children under 18 do not transact

Children under 18 do not pay Vetuonglai directly. Family Passport and Kids products are paid by the parent or guardian, with COPPA-compliant verifiable parental consent (US users) and Vietnam Luật Trẻ em 2016 compliance (Vietnamese users). Children under 13 may not create an account; ages 13-17 require parent-linked account.

### 2.4 Không công bố ROI / lợi nhuận kỳ vọng cho invest / No public expected returns

`invest.vetuonglai.com` and any public page that mentions investment do not state expected returns, ROI, target IRR, projected revenue multiples, or any forward-looking financial figure. Numbers exist only inside the PPM provided to a verified accredited / qualified investor after 90-day stickiness, KYC, AML, and a signed Investment Risk Disclosure.

### 2.5 Không bán dữ liệu thành viên / No sale of member data

Member data is never sold, brokered, or rented to a third party. Data leaves the system only with explicit, per-purpose, revocable member consent, or under lawful legal process. Member-data export (DSAR) and deletion are honored within the windows mandated by GDPR (where applicable), CCPA (California), and the Vietnamese privacy regime.

### 2.6 Where to display these 5

- `docs.vetuonglai.com/about/` — full text VI + EN.
- Homepage trust block — link to the docs page, plus a one-line distillation: *"5 cam kết: không hứa thu nhập • giới thiệu 1 cấp • không thu phí trẻ em • không công bố ROI • không bán dữ liệu."*
- Member welcome email — full list, post-signup.
- Every contributor application page — full list, with click-confirm.

These 5 must appear before any "join", "apply", "upgrade" CTA on any contributor or invest surface.

## 3. Forbidden vs preferred public lexicon

This section is binding for every public surface (homepage, docs, edu landing, kids landing, read landing, vc landing, pricing, all blog posts published under the brand, all email broadcasts, all social posts).

### 3.1 Forbidden public strings

The following exact strings (case-insensitive, with or without diacritics) are forbidden on any public surface:

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| `kiếm tiền` | Leads with money, not value. |
| `thu nhập thụ động` / `passive income` | Implies effortless income; FTC red flag. |
| `đổi đời` | Aspirational income promise. |
| `làm giàu` / `get rich` | Income promise. |
| `đảm bảo thu nhập` / `guaranteed income` | Income promise. |
| `earn $X / month` (any specific figure on public surface) | Income figure on public surface. |
| `affiliate` / `affiliate program` / `affiliate marketing` | FTC + Vietnam regulators treat this term as a red flag. Public uses replacements; see §3.2. |
| `MLM` / `multi-level` / `network marketing` / `downline` | Pyramid signal. |
| `marketplace` (as standalone product label) | Replaced by **Contributor Studio**. The string `marketplace` may appear inside long-form explanations of why we do not run an open-trade marketplace, but never as a product label. |
| `ROI` / `expected return` / `lợi nhuận kỳ vọng` | Forbidden on public invest surface. |
| `Invest now` / `Đầu tư ngay` / `Become an investor` / `Trở thành nhà đầu tư` | No invest CTA on public surface. |
| `Cơ hội đầu tư` (as CTA wording) | Forbidden as CTA; allowed only inside docs body prose with the gate explanation adjacent. |
| `Join investor waitlist` / `Đăng ký danh sách nhà đầu tư` | No waitlist capture on public surface. |
| `cheap labor` / `low-cost outsource` / `nhân công giá rẻ` | Forbidden Vietnam Advantage framing. |
| `iai.one` (any subdomain) | Old domain, never linked. |

### 3.2 Required replacements

When the underlying concept must be expressed in public, the only acceptable rendering is from this table:

| Concept | VI public-safe | EN public-safe |
|---|---|---|
| Earning from contribution | tạo giá trị / được trả công xứng đáng cho đóng góp | value creation / fair payout for contribution |
| Affiliate | giới thiệu minh bạch (1 cấp) | transparent referral (single-tier) |
| Affiliate program | chương trình đóng góp / contributor program | contributor program |
| Affiliate candidate | ứng viên đóng góp / contributor candidate | contributor candidate |
| Marketplace (as product) | Xưởng Đóng góp / Contributor Studio | Contributor Studio |
| Investment opportunity (in long-form prose only, never CTA) | giáo dục đầu tư và rủi ro | investment education and risk |
| Income range (post-login only + disclaimer) | khoảng thu nhập tham khảo (từ thành viên đã xác minh, không cam kết) | reference income range (from verified members, not a guarantee) |
| Referral revenue share (single-tier) | chia sẻ doanh thu giới thiệu (1 cấp) | referral revenue share (single-tier) |
| Material connection disclosure | tiết lộ quan hệ lợi ích | material connection disclosure |
| Invest gate | cổng giáo dục đầu tư có điều kiện | gated investment education |

### 3.3 Internal vs render layer

The codebase keeps internal identifiers (e.g. `affiliate_single_tier`, `affiliate_candidate`, `marketplace_listing`) for schema stability. **The render layer must always translate** to the public-safe wording in §3.2 before the string reaches a public surface. This is a build-gate concern: any HTML diff that ships a forbidden string must fail the release gate.

A `verify-public-lexicon.mjs` script (to be built by Team 5) reads `dist/` after build and fails the gate if it finds any string from §3.1 in a publicly-served HTML file. Until the script exists, manual review of `dist/**/*.html` is mandatory before each public deploy.

## 4. Vietnam Advantage — public framing

### 4.1 Allowed framing

Vietnam Advantage is publicly framed as **"Bản sắc vận hành Việt Nam" / "Operational fluency in Vietnam"**:

- Bilingual UX VI/EN end-to-end, with VI as the primary editorial standard for original content.
- Local payment rails: PayOS, domestic bank transfer, e-wallet (MoMo, ZaloPay, ViettelPay) — all with Nghị định 117/2025 e-commerce + tax withholding compliance.
- Legal compliance with Vietnamese regimes that protect users: Luật Bảo vệ người tiêu dùng, Nghị định 40/2018 (single-tier rule), Luật Trẻ em 2016 (Kids), Luật Chứng khoán (invest), Nghị định 13/2023 (data privacy).
- Vietnamese-language editorial standards on `read.vetuonglai.com`: the Read editorial board enforces Vietnamese stylistic clarity, not translated-from-English voice.
- A network of Vietnam-based contributors who can produce in Vietnamese without translation lag, and who understand Vietnamese regulatory context (tax, IP, family law, education law).

### 4.2 Forbidden framing

- "Cheap labor", "low-cost outsource", "nhân công giá rẻ", "rẻ hơn US", "Vietnam-priced".
- "Arbitrage" or "geographic arbitrage" as a value prop.
- Any framing that treats Vietnam contributors as cost savings rather than capability.
- Comparative pricing language that names another country negatively.

### 4.3 Where to display

- `docs.vetuonglai.com/about/` section "Why Vietnam-rooted".
- `docs.vetuonglai.com/contributor/` — to set expectations for Vietnam-based applicants.
- `read.vetuonglai.com/transparency/` — to explain editorial standards.
- Investor-facing materials (post-gate, inside PPM) — separate language allowed there because the audience is verified.

## 6. Public surface vs post-login surface boundary

This section is the single source of truth for "what may be shown without login" vs "what requires login".

### 6.1 Public surface (no login required)

Allowed:

- Brand statement, tagline, the 5 red lines (one-line + link to full).
- Free Member sign-up CTA.
- Annual Member Passport price ($99/year, with the 30% Y1 launch discount note while it applies).
- Mini products grid ($3-$9) with price visible.
- Edu program list (titles + outline only; no income claim, no completion-rate claim).
- VC explainer.
- Read book and essay landing pages with neutral pricing visible.
- `docs.vetuonglai.com` full content as operating manual (legal, FAQ, contributor explainer, invest education, etc.).
- One-line summary of what each product line does.
- Bilingual VI/EN switcher.

Forbidden on public surface (above OR below the fold):

- Any specific contributor income figure ("$1,000-$5,000/month", "top 10% earn $X").
- Any invest CTA or waitlist.
- Any expected return / ROI / forward financial figure.
- Family Passport and Partner Passport pricing on the V1 homepage above-the-fold (allowed inside `/pricing/` page detail, not as homepage hero).
- Contributor sign-up CTA in V1 (allowed in V2 once Builder-tier funnel exists).

### 6.2 Post-login surface (member-authenticated only)

Allowed (subject to the rules that apply per surface):

- Historical contributor income tier ranges with Income Disclaimer adjacent.
- Contributor application (if member has reached Builder tier).
- Invest education content + the 7 hard conditions checklist (without numerical returns).
- Personalized learning dashboard, credentials wallet, refund tracker, referral link generator (single-tier).

Still forbidden even post-login:

- Promised future returns on invest.
- Multi-tier referral structure (does not exist).
- Selling another member's data.

### 6.3 PPM surface (post-KYC verified investor only)

Allowed:

- Numerical projections, terms, risk factors, dilution model, exit scenarios — all inside the PPM document, with securities counsel co-signature.

This surface is not a webpage; it is a document delivered by the legal/finance team after gate clearance.

## 10. Bilingual VI/EN rules

### 10.1 Equal treatment

VI and EN are equal first-class languages on every public surface. Every page must be available in both, with a visible language switcher. The default language is detected from `Accept-Language`, then user preference, then VI.

### 10.2 VI is the editorial primary

For original content (not translated marketing), VI is written first to Vietnamese stylistic standards, then EN is produced. This means:

- VI is not "translated from English"; it has its own voice.
- EN is not "machine-translated from VI"; it has a human editor pass.
- When the two diverge in nuance, the VI is the source of truth for intent; the EN may rephrase.

### 10.3 Forbidden bilingual practices

- Mixing VI and EN in the same headline (forbidden: "Become a Thành viên").
- Auto-translation outputs shipped without human review.
- VI text rendered without diacritics on a public surface (use diacritics correctly; Vietnamese without diacritics is acceptable only inside code identifiers).

## 13. The "operating manual" posture of `docs.vetuonglai.com`

Per Memo §1.8: docs is the trust artifact, not a sales surface.

### 13.1 Tone

- Plain, direct, neutral.
- No urgency, no scarcity, no aspirational copy.
- Bilingual; VI primary editorial.
- No CTAs except "Đăng ký miễn phí" / "Tìm hiểu thêm" / "Đăng nhập".

### 13.2 Required sections (canonical sitemap)

- `/` — what Vetuonglai is, what it isn't (§1.4), the 5 commitments (§2), tagline.
- `/about/` — full brand statement, Vietnam Advantage framing, leadership.
- `/products/` — neutral one-line description per product, link to subdomain.
- `/membership/` — Member, Family, Partner Passports explained.
- `/pricing/` — full price detail (no hero scarcity).
- `/contributor/` — contributor program (single-tier referral, Builder gate, payout terms).
- `/income/` — Income Disclaimer in full + historical reference ranges (post-login link gate).
- `/invest/` — invest education only (§8).
- `/kids/` — COPPA + Luật Trẻ em explanation, parent consent flow.
- `/trust-system/` — pathway, tier, VC, transparency model.
- `/verification/` — VC + Builder gate.
- `/work/` — career profile + services lite (Member capability).
- `/legal/` — full list of 12 canonical legal documents (per Memo §6.4 + product architecture doc).
- `/legal/material-connection/` — FTC disclosure standard (canonical, no fallback `/legal/affiliate/`).
- `/legal/income/` — Income Disclaimer.
- `/legal/invest/` — Investment Risk Disclosure full text.
- `/legal/kids/` — COPPA + Luật Trẻ em statements.
- `/legal/privacy/` — privacy policy.
- `/legal/cookies/`, `/legal/tos/`, `/legal/refund/`, `/legal/contractor/`, `/legal/spam/`, `/legal/dmca/`, `/legal/conduct/`.
- `/faq/` — FAQ (no income claim, no invest claim above gate).
- `/getting-started/` — first 7-day checklist for a new member.
- `/changelog/` — version log of policy changes (transparency).
- `/support/` — contact, refund flow.

### 13.3 What docs must not do

- Sell.
- Push toward upgrade urgency.
- Hide forbidden language inside a "fine print" section to bypass the lexicon rule.
- Link to invest with any urgency wording.

## 14. Definition of Done — public positioning layer

The public positioning layer is "done for V1" when:

- ✅ Tagline (§1.2) is rendered identically across homepage, docs footer, OG image, welcome email.
- ✅ The 5 red lines (§2) are rendered identically across `/about/`, homepage trust block, contributor application, every email footer that references earning or referral.
- ✅ Forbidden strings (§3.1) audit returns zero hits in `dist/`.
- ✅ Required replacements (§3.2) are the only renderings of the underlying concepts in `dist/`.
- ✅ Vietnam Advantage framing (§4) appears as "operational fluency" wording, not "cheap labor".
- ✅ Pre-V1 legal opinions (§5.1) are on file.
- ✅ Public/post-login boundary (§6) is enforced by route-level gating.
- ✅ Microcopy library (§7) is the only source for public CTAs.
- ✅ Invest surface (§8) follows the off-nav, education-only, no-CTA, no-waitlist rules.
- ✅ Kids public-surface rules (§9) are enforced.
- ✅ Bilingual VI/EN parity (§10) holds on every public page.
- ✅ Email and social rules (§11, §12) are documented in team handbooks; brand-account bios match.
- ✅ Docs site sitemap (§13.2) is live with neutral tone (§13.1).
- ✅ Quarterly compliance audit (§5.5) is scheduled and Q1 owner assigned.

When all 14 boxes pass, this layer is V1-complete. V2 expansion (Family Passport public CTA, contributor public sign-up post-Builder funnel) requires a Founder go-decision separate from this doc.
