# READ_CREATOR_04 — Editorial Review and Anti-Fraud Policy

## Muc tieu

Bao ve chat luong noi dung va ngan cac hanh vi gian lan payout.

## Editorial review

Luong co ban:

- submit
- intake review
- editorial review
- approve / reject / request changes
- publish log

## Anti-fraud controls

- duplicate content detection
- self-referral detection
- suspicious payout review
- role conflict review
- manual hold for abnormal refund or traffic spike

## Stop-line cases

- content dao van
- gia mao contributor
- split claim khong co approval
- referrer tu tao giao dich de an hoa hong
- review queue bi thao tung de dua bai len public
