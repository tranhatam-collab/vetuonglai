# READ_CREATOR_03 — Onboarding, KYC, and Tax Flow

## Scope

File nay mo ta flow onboarding creator theo role-based contribution system.

## Role onboarding order

V1 uu tien:

- Author
- Reviewer

V2 mo rong:

- Translator
- Editor
- Designer
- Referrer
- Curator
- Sponsor

## KYC flow

Bat buoc truoc payout:

- legal name
- payout identity type
- bank account
- residency / tax status
- required contract acceptance

Khong public cac truong nay.

## Tax flow

Tax khong duoc khoa cung theo 1 ty le duy nhat cho moi case.

Decision inputs:

- ca nhan / ho kinh doanh / doanh nghiep
- loai thu nhap
- loai san pham
- hop dong ap dung
- quy dinh dang co hieu luc

## Stop-line

Khong bat auto-withholding tren production neu chua co legal/accounting signoff.
