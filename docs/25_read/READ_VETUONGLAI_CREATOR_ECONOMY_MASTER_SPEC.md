# READ VETUONGLAI CREATOR ECONOMY MASTER SPEC

## Status

Approved with legal and provider verification required before public launch.

## Public promise

`read.vetuonglai.com` la lop doc, viet, dich, bien tap va chia se tri thuc trong he Ve Tuong Lai.

Cau cong bo chuan:

- Ai tao ra gia tri, nguoi do duoc ghi nhan.
- Moi khoan chia doanh thu phai co cong thuc ro rang, co lich tra ro rang, va co bao cao minh bach.

Khong duoc dung:

- ai cung kiem tien
- thu nhap thu dong
- dam bao duoc tra
- PayOS mien phi vinh vien
- platform khau tru dung moi loai thue

## Strategic model

Giu 8 vai tro:

- Author
- Translator
- Editor
- Reviewer
- Curator
- Referrer
- Designer
- Sponsor

Public framing:

- dung `role-based contribution system`
- khong dung `ai cung kiem tien`

## Revenue split soft lock

Platform operating fee:

- target: khong qua 15%
- public wording: muc phi van hanh duoc cong bo truoc moi giao dich
- neu muon khoa `toi da 15%`, phai dua vao Terms va co versioning

Creator share:

- target 70-85% tuy san pham va vai tro
- hien thi truoc khi publish va truoc khi ban

Payment fee:

- lay theo phi thuc te cua provider tai thoi diem giao dich
- neu provider dang 0 phi, chi hien thi 0 tai checkout/ledger o thoi diem ap dung

Tax:

- tinh theo loai thu nhap, creator status, hop dong, loai san pham, va rule dang ap dung
- legal/accounting phai xac nhan truoc khi tu dong khau tru

## Transparency lock

Khong public public ledger theo cach co the lo du lieu tai chinh ca nhan.

Chi duoc mo:

- anonymized ledger
- aggregate summary
- hash-based reference
- quarter reports khong lo danh tinh

Khong duoc mo:

- ten that
- tai khoan ngan hang
- CCCD
- ma so thue
- so tien ca nhan neu co the suy luan danh tinh
