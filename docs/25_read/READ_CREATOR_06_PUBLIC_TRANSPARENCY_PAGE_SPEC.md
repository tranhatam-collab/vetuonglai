# READ_CREATOR_06 — Public Transparency Page Spec

## Muc tieu

Cho public thay duoc co che minh bach ma khong lo du lieu tai chinh ca nhan.

## Duoc hien thi

- tong gross theo ky
- tong creator pool theo ky
- tong platform fee theo ky
- tong provider fee theo ky
- tong tax withheld theo nhom
- hash reference cho ledger entries
- report quarter

## Khong duoc hien thi

- ten creator that
- exact payout cua tung ca nhan
- bank details
- CCCD / MST
- bat ky data nao co the suy luan danh tinh tai chinh

## Public wording

Dung:

- minh bach tong hop
- du lieu da an danh
- so lieu co the doi theo chinh sach va quy dinh ap dung

Khong dung:

- public ledger live day du cho moi creator
- chung toi cong khai toan bo giao dich ca nhan
