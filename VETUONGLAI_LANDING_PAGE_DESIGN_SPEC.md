# VETUONGLAI_LANDING_PAGE_DESIGN_SPEC

## Muc tieu

Trang chu khong phai de "gioi thieu".

Trang chu phai giup nguoi dung:
- hieu ngay he Ve Tuong Lai la gi
- thay minh trong he nay
- bat dau hanh trinh ngay lap tuc

## Nguyen tac nen tang

- Future Calm: sang, tho, on dinh, co chieu sau
- Human-centered: bat dau tu nguoi that, van de that
- Experience-first: uu tien trai nghiem va hanh dong
- Khong LMS cu, khong startup UI generic

## Cau truc trang bat buoc

1. Hero
2. Problem section
3. Vision section
4. Ecosystem map
5. Journey entry
6. Proof / Stories
7. CTA cuoi

## 1) Hero

### Noi dung

- Headline: "Mot tuong lai tot hon bat dau tu hom nay"
- Sub: "Khong phai hoc de thi, ma hoc de song, lam, va tro thanh chinh minh"

### CTA

- Bat dau hanh trinh
- Kham pha he

### Visual direction

- gradient sang, mem
- anh sang lan toa
- hinh anh con nguoi that
- typography ro rang, co nhip tho

## 2) Problem Section

### Noi dung cot loi

Con nguoi hien tai dang:
- mat phuong huong
- hoc sai cach
- song sai nhip
- kiem tien khong ben

### UI

- 3-5 problem cards
- moi card = 1 van de + 1 he qua + 1 dau hieu de nhan dien

## 3) Vision Section

### Noi dung cot loi

Tuong lai can huong toi:
- song du day
- tu chu
- hieu ban than
- song co gia tri

### UI

- layout mo, khong box cung
- su dung khoang trang de tao cam giac nhe va sang

## 4) Ecosystem Map

### Module bat buoc

- Life
- Edu
- Finance
- Invest
- Green
- VC
- Community

### UI

- hien thi dang network / map
- hover vao tung module de xem preview nhanh: vai tro, dau ra, bang chung

## 5) Journey Entry

### Flow

"Ban dang o dau?"
-> nguoi dung chon tinh trang hien tai
-> he goi y hanh trinh phu hop

### Yeu cau

- toi da 3 buoc de vao hanh trinh
- tao duoc cam giac "duoc dan duong", khong bi qua tai

## 6) Proof / Stories

### Noi dung

- nguoi that
- chuyen hoa that
- ket qua co the kiem chung

### UI

- story cards co cau truc ngan gon: truoc -> hanh dong -> ket qua -> bang chung
- tuyet doi tranh copy marketing khoa truong

## 7) CTA Cuoi

Thong diep bat buoc:
- "Bat dau tu hom nay"

CTA phu hop theo hanh trinh:
- vao onboarding
- vao EDU
- vao challenge dau tien

## Design Rules

- spacing rong, thoang
- text de doc, contrast ro
- tranh overload noi dung
- uu tien luong doc tu nhien tren mobile va desktop
- animation nhe, co y nghia, khong phan tam

## Runtime Truth Checklist

Moi khoi trong landing page phai tra loi duoc:
- Day la UI hay da tao hanh dong that?
- User co the vao hanh trinh that ngay tu khoi nay khong?
- Hanh dong nay tao ra ket qua gi?
- Ket qua do duoc xac minh nhu the nao?

Neu chua tra loi du 4 cau hoi -> chua duoc coi la done.
