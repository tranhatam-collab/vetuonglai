1. Scope

This policy covers all personal data the Service collects when users access, register for, or use the platform. The Service complies with Vietnam's Cybersecurity Law and Decree 13/2023/ND-CP, and with the GDPR for European Union users.

2. Data Collected

We collect: (a) account data (email, identifiers, hashed passwords); (b) behavioural data (access logs, product events); (c) financial data for paid tiers (transaction IDs, invoices; full card numbers are not stored).

3. Legal Bases

Processing is performed on the bases of: contract performance, the Service's legitimate interest in operating security and fraud prevention, user consent for ancillary purposes, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Retention

Financial data is retained for at least seven (7) years pursuant to accounting requirements. Inactive accounts are reviewed after twenty-four (24) months and may be anonymised or deleted under minimal-retention policy.

5. Third Parties

We share limited data with processors: PayOS (payments), Cloudflare (infrastructure and security), and our transactional email vendor. Each processor is bound by a data processing agreement.

6. Data Residency

Primary storage is in Vietnam. For EU users, additional GDPR-aligned safeguards (including Schrems II considerations) apply to any cross-border transfer.

7. Data Subject Rights

Users have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. Requests may be sent to privacy@vetuonglai.com and will be addressed within the periods required by law.

8. DPO Contact

Privacy enquiries may be addressed to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@vetuonglai.com.

Legal contact: legal@vetuonglai.com
