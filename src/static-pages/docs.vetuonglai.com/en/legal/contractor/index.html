Contractor Terms

1. Nature of Relationship

Contributors work under an independent services contract and are not employees of the Service. Contributors do not receive employment benefits or protections.

2. Tax Responsibility

Contributors are solely responsible for declaring and paying income tax under the law of their tax residence. The Service may withhold at source where law requires and shall issue corresponding documentation.

3. Payment Cadence

Contributor payments follow the regular cycle published in the contributor dashboard. The Service may suspend payments where policy violation is suspected.

4. Work Product Rights

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, contributors grant the Service a non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual licence to use the work for the operation of the Service. Contributors retain the right to use the work in their personal portfolio.

5. Confidentiality

Contributors agree not to disclose internal information, customer data, source code, or technical know-how. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of the contractor relationship.

6. Code of Conduct

Contributors comply with the general Code of Conduct and the contributor-specific Code of Conduct (to be issued separately before GA), including prohibitions on harassment, discrimination, fraud, and undisclosed conflict of interest.

7. Termination

Either party may terminate with appropriate notice. Rights and obligations accrued before termination shall survive.
