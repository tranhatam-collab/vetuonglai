1. Scope

This policy applies to transactions made directly with Vetuonglai. For purchases through resellers, users follow the reseller's policy where it differs.

2. 90-Day Window for Annual Member

Users on annual Member plans may request a full 100% refund within ninety (90) days of the first payment, with no reason required.

3. 30-Day Window for Mini

Mini plans benefit from a thirty (30) day refund window from the date of payment.

4. Partial Refunds

After the full window closes, certain plans may be refunded pro-rata for the unused period, less a fixed processing fee, in accordance with an appendix published on the relevant product page.

5. Non-Refundable Items

Digital content already downloaded or consumed, and one-off services already delivered (such as a completed consultation), are not eligible for refund.

6. Request Process

Users email billing@vetuonglai.com with their order ID. We respond within five (5) business days; approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within fourteen (14) business days of approval.

7. Abuse

We reserve the right to decline refund requests showing signs of abuse, such as repeated re-subscription to exploit the refund window, or payment fraud.
