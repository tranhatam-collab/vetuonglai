Material Connection Disclosure

Version 2026.05.07

DRAFT — Skeleton version 2026.05.07 pending full legal review before GA.

1. Purpose

This page discloses the categories of material connections that may arise when a person refers Vetuonglai to others or participates in the contributor program. It is required by FTC 16 CFR §255, the EU Platform Work Directive 2024/2831, and Vietnamese law on multi-level marketing.

2. Anti-MLM Statement

The Service does not constitute multi-level marketing and has no tiered commission structure. All referrals are single-tier: the referrer receives a benefit only from the person they directly referred. There is no second level, no downline, and no commission tree.

3. When Disclosure Applies

A clear and conspicuous disclosure must appear when a member sends a referral link by message, email, or social media; when a contributor publishes content that mentions the Service and includes a link; when a banner, widget, or product UI displays a referral code; or in any other case where a material benefit is received in connection with the Service.

4. Standard Wording

Default: "This link may provide a benefit to the person who shared it. You do not pay extra when you sign up through this link." Short variant for video overlay or short caption: "Referral benefit applies — no extra cost to you."

5. Placement Rules

Place the disclosure near the link, in the same sentence or the immediately adjacent one. Show before or alongside the call-to-action, never after. Do not hide it in the footer, terms, privacy, or below the fold. Use a font size at least equal to body text and avoid background-matching colours. On video, hold on-screen text for at least three seconds and add a verbal mention when voice-over is present.

6. Social Media

On TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn, the caption must open or close with the disclosure. Hashtags such as #ad or #sponsored alone do not replace a disclosure. Stories and reels must include on-screen overlay text for the entire time the link is visible.

7. No Income Claims

Wording such as "guaranteed income", "easy money", "life-changing income", "financial freedom", "passive income", or specific investment returns is prohibited. Any income reference may appear only after the user has signed in and accepted the Income Disclaimer.

8. Jurisdictions

United States — FTC 16 CFR §255 Endorsement Guides. European Union — Platform Work Directive 2024/2831 (algorithmic transparency, no fake-contractor classification, human review). Vietnam — Decree 40/2018 and amendments: single-tier referrals only; tiered commission structures are prohibited.

9. Consequences of Non-Compliance

Content lacking a disclosure will be removed from the Service. The referral code of the responsible user may be temporarily disabled or permanently revoked. In severe cases (income claims, public investment solicitation, impersonation of the Service), the account may be closed and payouts withheld under policy. Violations may also trigger sanctions from the FTC, EU regulators, or Vietnamese authorities.
