Kids Account Terms

Version 2026.05.07

DRAFT — Skeleton version 2026.05.07 pending full legal review before GA.

1. Age Range

Kids accounts are available to users aged six (6) to seventeen (17). Users under 6 may not create an account. Users aged 18 or older use the standard adult account.

2. Parent Management

Every kids account must be linked to a parent or legal guardian account. The parent is responsible for providing consent under COPPA and Vietnamese law, and for managing access rights.

3. Tier 1-2 Cap

Kids accounts have access only to Tier 1 and Tier 2. Higher tiers require the user to be at least 18 and are not available to kids accounts.

4. No Earnings

Kids accounts do not participate in any earnings, commission, or payout programme. All money-related features are disabled.

5. No Public Profile

Kids accounts have no public profile, do not appear on leaderboards, and are not issued referral codes. Social interaction is limited to moderated environments.

6. COPPA Alignment

The Service complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act and equivalent child-protection rules. Children's personal data is processed at the minimum necessary level and is not used for behavioural advertising.

7. Consent Withdrawal

Parents may withdraw consent at any time, leading to closure of the account and deletion of the child's personal data under our minimal-retention policy.
