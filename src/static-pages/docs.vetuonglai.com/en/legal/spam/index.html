Anti-Spam Policy

Version 2026.05.07

DRAFT — Skeleton version 2026.05.07 pending full legal review before GA.

1. General Principle

The Service does not tolerate spam. Users must not send unsolicited promotional messages, nor promote third-party products through Service communication channels without authorisation.

2. User-to-User Messaging

User-to-user messaging requires minimal mutual context (connected, prior transaction, or recipient-initiated). Recipients may always block and report.

3. Rate Limits

Automated rate limits apply to messages, invitations, and referral links. Exceeding thresholds may trigger temporary blocks and manual review.

4. Reporting and Investigation

Users may report spam via in-product controls or to abuse@vetuonglai.com. The Service investigates within five (5) business days.

5. Consequences

A three-step ladder applies: (a) warning; (b) temporary suspension; (c) permanent ban. For mass-fraud cases, the Service may bypass warnings and apply an immediate ban.

6. Legal Scope

This policy aligns with Vietnam's Cybersecurity Law, the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act, and the GDPR/PECR for EU/UK users.
