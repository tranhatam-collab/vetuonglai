1. No Guarantee

The Service makes no promise, guarantee, or projection of income for any user, contributor, or referrer. All public content is educational and illustrative.

2. High Variability

Financial outcomes from participating in the ecosystem depend on individual skill, time invested, market conditions, and many factors outside the Service's control. A significant portion of participants may earn nothing.

3. Conditional Income Reports

Detailed income reports, if available, are shown only after the user has signed in and acknowledged this disclaimer. No public surface presents headline average-income figures as promises.

4. No Public Projections

The Service does not publish income projections on public pages. Any illustrative simulation is clearly labelled and is not a commitment of results.

5. Prohibited Claims

Contributors, ambassadors, and partners are prohibited from making statements that imply income guarantees, such as "guaranteed income", "change your life", or "financial freedom in X months". Violations are addressed under the Code of Conduct.

6. Additional Warning

Users are encouraged to consult an independent financial professional before making personal financial decisions based on their own circumstances and goals.
