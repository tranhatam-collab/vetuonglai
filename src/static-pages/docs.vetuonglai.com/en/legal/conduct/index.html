Code of Conduct

Version 2026.05.07

DRAFT — Skeleton version 2026.05.07 pending full legal review before GA.

1. Respect Principle

All members are expected to behave respectfully, civilly, and constructively across the Service's public and semi-public spaces.

2. Prohibited Conduct

The following are prohibited: targeted harassment; discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristic; disclosure of another person's private information (doxxing); threats of violence; fraud, impersonation, and deception.

3. Enforcement Levels

A four-step ladder applies: (a) reminder; (b) formal warning; (c) timed suspension; (d) account termination. Severity is determined by the gravity of the conduct and prior history.

4. Appeal Process

A user under enforcement may submit a written appeal to trust@vetuonglai.com within fourteen (14) days. The Service responds within ten (10) business days.

5. Contributors

Contributors additionally observe a contributor-specific Code of Conduct (to be published before GA), emphasising conflict of interest, disclosure of material connections, and content transparency.

6. Reporter Protection

Good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation. Retaliation reports are addressed at the highest tier under Section 3.

7. Contact

Report violations to trust@vetuonglai.com or through in-product reporting controls.

Legal contact: legal@vetuonglai.com
