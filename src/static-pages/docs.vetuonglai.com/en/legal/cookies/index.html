1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files stored on a user's device used for essential functions, preference memory, or operational measurement. This policy also applies to equivalent technologies including localStorage and tracking pixels.

2. Categories

We classify cookies into three categories: (a) essential, which cannot be disabled; (b) functional, which remember user preferences; (c) analytics, which are OFF by default and only enabled after explicit consent.

3. Retention

Session cookies expire when the browser is closed. Essential cookies last up to twelve (12) months. Analytics cookies, if accepted, last up to thirteen (13) months in line with CNIL guidance.

4. Account Linking

When a user is signed in, cookie identifiers may be linked with the account for lawful personalisation and fraud prevention. Users may request unlinking via privacy@vetuonglai.com.

5. User Controls

Users may refuse, delete, or reset cookies through their browser controls and through the on-site cookie preferences panel. Declining non-essential cookies does not impair core functionality.

6. Updates

When the cookie inventory changes, this page will be updated and renewed consent will be requested where consent is the basis.
