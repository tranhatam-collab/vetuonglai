- decorative

- premium

## 2. Final symbol decision

### Chosen symbol system
The approved mark keeps the **open circle + V-check** logic from the locked brand library, but refines it to be more premium and less generic. fileciteturn16file0

### What changed
1. Circle is thinner and cleaner
2. Opening in the circle is smaller and more intentional
3. V-check is sharper and more geometric
4. Wordmark spacing is calmer
5. Overall composition is more restrained

### Why this is stronger
- easier to recognize at favicon size
- cleaner on print
- feels more international
- reduces the risk of looking like a “community startup badge”
- works better across web, deck, print, and app avatar
